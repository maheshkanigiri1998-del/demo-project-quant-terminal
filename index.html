📐 DCF Valuation▾
How DCF works: Values a stock as the sum of all future Free Cash Flows, discounted back to today.

WACC = blended cost of capital. Typical: US 8–10%, India 12–15%, UK 9–11%.
Growth Y1-5 = near-term FCF growth. Use revenue CAGR as proxy.
Terminal Growth = perpetual rate ≈ long-run GDP (3% US, 6% India).

⚠ WACC ±1% shifts valuation ±15%. Treat as a range, not a price target.
